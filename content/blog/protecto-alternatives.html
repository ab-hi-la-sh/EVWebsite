The best Protecto alternatives for 2026: 1. Enigma Vault (NoPII), 2. Limina, 3. Grepture, 4. Protegrity, 5. Skyflow, 6. Nightfall AI.

Protecto is an AI data control plane that finds sensitive data and masks it before it reaches LLMs, AI agents, RAG pipelines and MCP tools, and G2 lists it under Data Masking, Sensitive Data Discovery, Data Privacy Management and AI Security Solutions. Across the G2 reviews of the alternatives below, buyers keep coming back to the same points: Ease of use and setup (Ease of Use is Nightfall AI’s most-mentioned strength on G2, with 12 mentions), detection accuracy, quality of support, and price.

The best overall alternative to Protecto is Enigma Vault (NoPII), an LLM privacy proxy that tokenizes PII before it reaches the model provider and restores it in the response, free for one million protected tokens a month. Other well-known alternatives to Protecto are:

  • Limina (formerly Private AI): Best suited to on-prem de-identification in 52 languages (G2 rating 4.6 from 22 reviews)
  • Nightfall AI: Offers data loss prevention that stops employees and agents leaking data into AI tools (G2 rating 4.6 from 101 reviews)
  • Protegrity: Best for enterprise tokenization across AI and analytics pipelines (G2 rating 4.5 from 14 reviews)
  • Grepture: Known for a low-cost, EU-hosted AI gateway for small teams (no G2 reviews yet)

Every rating in this guide links to the product’s G2 or Capterra page, so you can read the verified reviews behind each like, dislike and score for yourself.

Why Teams Look for Protecto Alternatives

Protecto has real strengths: Broad entity coverage, fully air-gapped deployment, and published SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and a HIPAA BAA. These are the four reasons buyers most often look elsewhere.

NoPII is an LLM privacy proxy from Enigma Vault. It detects sensitive values in each request, tokenizes them before the request reaches the model provider, and restores the real values in the response, so your application and your users never see a token.

For a Protecto buyer the difference is how much you build. Protecto gives you APIs and an SDK to mask and unmask data inside your own code. NoPII sits in the request path: You change your model client’s base URL, and detection, tokenization, forwarding and restoration happen in the proxy.

The trade is deployment. NoPII is a hosted proxy, so teams that must keep processing inside their own VPC or an air-gapped network are better served by Protecto or Limina.

Key features

  • Base URL swap: Point your existing OpenAI-style client at the NoPII endpoint; no new SDK or middleware
  • Consistent tokens: The same value gets the same token every time, so multi-turn conversations and reasoning stay coherent
  • Detection: 30+ configurable entity types, plus 14 families of secrets and API keys caught in the same pass
  • Streaming: Standard and SSE responses are restored on the fly as chunks arrive
  • Fail-safe by default: If tokenization fails, the request is blocked rather than forwarded
  • Context phrase neutralization: Phrases that trigger model refusals on tokenized text are handled in the proxy
  • Token retention: Free tier tokens expire after one day; Pro and Enterprise set retention from one day to permanent
  • 9+ model providers under one proxy, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq, Together and Fireworks

How to Choose a Protecto Alternative

Answer these questions in order. Each one narrows the list.

1. Do you need to protect the prompts your own product sends to an LLM? Choose a layer that sits in front of those calls: Enigma Vault’s NoPII, a privacy proxy that tokenizes customer details before they reach the model and puts them back in the answer, or Grepture’s gateway.
