What Is Credit Card Tokenization Software?

Credit card tokenization software swaps a card's primary account number (PAN) for a random token that is worthless if stolen, so a business can keep cards on file and charge them later while raw card data stays out of most of its PCI DSS scope.

The real number stays in the provider's secured vault and is swapped back in only when you charge through your gateway. Your acquirer or QSA confirms the final PCI scope.

What to Test in a Two-Week Trial

  1. Tokenize a test card through a hosted form and confirm your servers never log the number.
  2. Charge a tokenized card through your current gateway.
  3. Run the token through your checkout validation and fraud rules.
  4. Take a test card over the phone and confirm the number is absent from the recording.
  5. Reveal a full card with a restricted role and confirm the audit log records it.
  6. Ask for a sample export file and the steps to move cards out.
  7. Time the integration with one engineer, or one operations lead for a no-code workflow.
