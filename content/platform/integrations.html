Triplets and your retrieval stack.

Triplets is substrate-agnostic. It works alongside whatever vector database, graph, or pipeline you already run, rather than replacing them. It re-ingests your corpus into its own structured model of entities, relationships, and verified values, then governs answering at query time. Adopting it is not a migration.

  • ✓Any vector database or graph as the retrieval substrate
  • ✓Your existing ETL, connectors, and ingestion pipelines
  • ✓Your applications and agents, under your brand
  • ✓Provider-agnostic: it governs answers regardless of which model you call

NoPII integrations for LLM APIs and frameworks.

Point your existing SDK at the NoPII base URL and every prompt is protected on the way out and restored on the way back. Switch models, run several at once, or test next week’s release. Your privacy controls do not reset when your model choice changes.

Supported: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, and any OpenAI-compatible API, 9+ providers in all. Works with LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, LiteLLM, and the Vercel AI SDK, with Langfuse tracing for observability. 18 public integration examples on GitHub.

  • ✓SSE streaming supported natively, so real-time chat stays real-time
  • ✓Deterministic tokens keep multi-turn conversations coherent
  • ✓BAA available for healthcare workloads

Card Vault payment gateway integrations.

Payment capture and gateway routing live in Card Vault.

A developer-first REST API for taking sensitive data out of your systems without taking capability out of your product. Field encryption that keeps your queries working, file storage your app references by handle, and card tokenization that charges through the payment gateway you already use.

  • ✓Card Vault proxies transactions to any REST payment gateway, including Authorize.net, Stripe, Braintree, and PayPal
  • ✓Hosted and iframe payment forms in 13 languages, with custom CSS, plus Magento and WooCommerce support
  • ✓Phone payments through Twilio Pay IVR capture
  • ✓Webhooks, a live Swagger UI at api.enigmavault.io, and integration examples on GitHub
