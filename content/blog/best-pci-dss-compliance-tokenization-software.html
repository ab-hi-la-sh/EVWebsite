What Is PCI DSS Compliant Tokenization Software?

PCI DSS compliant tokenization software replaces card numbers (PANs) with tokens that are worthless if stolen and keeps the real numbers inside a provider's PCI-validated vault, so far fewer of your own systems fall under a PCI DSS assessment.

Your apps, databases, and logs store only tokens. The provider holds the card data and carries the controls for it, and your acquirer or QSA confirms what stays in scope.

How Tokenization Reduces PCI DSS Scope

The PCI Security Standards Council's tokenization guidance sets the rule: for tokens and the systems holding them to be out of scope, they must have no value to an attacker trying to retrieve the PAN. Three points from that guidance shape every buying decision.

  • The vault stays in scope: the tokenization system is part of the cardholder data environment, which is why handing it to a Level 1 provider moves that burden off your team.
  • Retrievable PAN keeps you in scope: if your environment can pull the PAN back, your environment is in scope.
  • Payment tokens need a check: tokens that can start a transaction may still be in scope, so confirm with your acquirer.

Source: PCI DSS Tokenization Guidelines, PCI Security Standards Council.

What your integration means for your questionnaire

Integration patternQuestionnaire it usually points toWhat it means for you
Card data enters a provider's iframe, hosted fields, or full redirectSAQ AThe smallest set of controls; your servers never touch the PAN
Card data passes through your own servers before tokenizationSAQ DThe full set of controls applies to your environment
Card-present payments through a PCI-listed P2PE solutionSAQ P2PEFewer requirements for in-person payments, following the solution's instruction manual

What changed for SAQ A in 2025: the SAQ A revision for PCI DSS v4.0.1 removed requirements 6.4.3 and 11.6.1 and added an eligibility criterion that the merchant's site is not susceptible to attacks from scripts. The PCI SSC FAQ 1588 says this applies when your page embeds a provider's payment form, and that written confirmation from a PCI DSS compliant provider about its embedded solution can help you meet it. Ask every vendor below for that confirmation.

Version note: PCI DSS v3.2.1 retired on 31 March 2024, and the future-dated requirements of v4.0 became mandatory after 31 March 2025, according to the PCI Security Standards Council.
