The Vault/Customer Vault

Stop collecting sensitive data through email.

Customer Vault is secure document collection and intake software for teams that handle sensitive customer information. It is a turnkey, branded web app that collects cards, documents, signatures, and sensitive fields through secure links your customers can use without an account. Everything arrives encrypted, audit-logged, and isolated per tenant. No developers, no integration, live the same day.

The product

Collect, manage and share customer information securely.

Customer management.

Search, filter, and manage every customer record from a single dashboard, with associated cards, files, and activity at a glance, plus bulk operations and pagination.

Customer profiles.

One centralized record per customer: tokenized cards, encrypted files, signatures, custom fields, and notes, all linked to a single profile.

Branded intake forms.

Customers submit cards, files, signatures, and sensitive fields through public links, no account or login required. Branded per tenant, with HTML sanitization on every submission and CAPTCHA protection against abuse.

Staff inbox.

A real-time feed of incoming submissions. Assign each to a new or existing customer profile, add internal notes, and track processing status from a shared team inbox.

Secure file sharing.

Share files through expiring links, 24-hour default, with optional password protection, download tracking, and a full audit trail. Every file encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Custom sensitive fields.

Define fields beyond payment cards, per tenant, automatically tokenized through the Data Vault. Presets for SSN, driver’s license, passport, date of birth, and tax ID, with custom pattern validation for anything else.

Electronic signatures.

Canvas-based signature capture through intake forms or directly, stored encrypted and linked to the customer record with a full audit trail.

Reveal, hide, delete.

Every sensitive value on a profile carries explicit controls, so staff see what they need and nothing more, and every reveal is logged.

Connected when you want.

Signed webhooks and a full lifecycle API let engineering wire intake into your systems later, without changing how your team works today.

How it runs

Three steps to secure customer data.

01

Create an intake link.

Generate a branded, public link. Your customers submit cards, upload files, and sign documents without creating an account.

02

Customer submits.

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Submissions appear in your staff inbox instantly.

03

Staff manages.

Your team reviews, assigns to profiles, shares files securely, and manages the relationship from there.

Security

Security your assessor will recognize.

WebAuthn passkeys.

Staff authenticate with FIDO2 passkeys. No passwords to steal, phish, or brute-force. Hardware-backed by default.

Enforced MFA.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every staff account, with TOTP as the fallback when passkeys are unavailable. There is no opt-out.

Tenant isolation.

Separate authentication, separate data partitions, separate encryption keys, and a tenant-branded interface with role-based access control.

PCI scope reduction.

Cards are tokenized immediately through the Card Vault. The full card number is never returned to your application, so payment data stays out of your CRM and databases entirely.

Activity audit logging.

Every user action logged with user ID, action type, timestamp, and affected resources. The compliance trail writes itself.

Encrypted at every layer.

Every file sealed with AES-256-GCM envelope encryption and every card tokenized the moment it enters the vault. Plaintext never touches your infrastructure.
