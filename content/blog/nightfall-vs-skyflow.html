Where Nightfall and Skyflow Differ in Practice

Employee Monitoring and SaaS Coverage

Nightfall’s endpoint agent sees what happens on a laptop: copy and paste into an AI chat, a file upload to a personal drive, a browser session in an unapproved tool. Its SaaS integrations scan content at rest and in motion inside apps such as Slack, Google Drive, Jira, and Salesforce. When it finds something, policy decides whether to block, redact, coach the user, or simply alert the security team.

Skyflow never looks at a laptop. Its work happens earlier, when data is collected and stored. If your support tool, warehouse, and analytics stack only ever receive tokens, there is far less raw data for an employee to paste anywhere, although data typed in by hand from another source stays outside its view.

AI Agents and MCP Servers

Both vendors added agent features in the past year, and they face opposite directions.

Nightfall Premier watches the developer’s machine: it scans or blocks prompts, MCP tool calls, tool responses, and shell commands in Cursor, Claude Code (IDE and CLI), and VS Code.

It discovers local stdio MCP servers, flags shadow MCP, inventories remote HTTP and SSE servers, and offers an MCP gateway that enforces data policy on tool traffic. Its pricing page says LLM model responses are monitor-only in the hooks.

Skyflow’s homepage now opens with the line “Agents break data security,” and positions the vault between datastores, models, and agents, governing data flowing over MCP, A2A, or APIs. Skyflow announced MCP data protection in August 2025 and launched Skyflow for Glean in August 2026.

In our reading of Skyflow’s earlier pages for the Protecto vs Skyflow comparison, several agent use cases, such as agent memory and agentic commerce, were marketing pages without matching documentation, so ask for a demo of your exact workflow.

Reversibility: Nightfall Redaction and Skyflow Re-Identification

This is the row that matters most for AI features inside your own product. Nightfall’s Developer API returns findings and a redacted copy of the text: masking, or substitution with a phrase or detector name. Those are one-way. Its only reversible option encrypts each finding with your RSA public key, and its docs leave decryption to you.

Skyflow keeps the original. De-identified text carries vault tokens, entity counters, or entity-only tags, and the re-identify call returns real values to the roles your policy allows.

That suits workflows where a person or downstream system must eventually act on the real data, such as sending a refund to the right customer after a model drafts the reply. The cost is code: your app calls de-identify before the model and re-identify after it.

Beyond Text: Files, Audio, and Training Data

Skyflow’s de-identification handles CSV, DOC, PPT, PDF (redaction only), audio, and images, and its AI Data Sanitization product replaces PII and business-identifying information in training data while keeping referential integrity, so the same customer stays the same pseudonym across tables.
