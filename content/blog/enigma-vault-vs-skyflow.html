You are comparing Enigma Vault and Skyflow because sensitive data is sitting somewhere it should not: card numbers in a CRM, passports in an inbox, or customer names flowing into LLM prompts. Both products move that data into a vault and give your systems a token instead.

The two are built for very different buyers. Skyflow is an enterprise data privacy vault that now describes itself as runtime AI data control for datastores, models, and agents, whereas Enigma Vault is a PCI DSS Level 1 tokenization platform with purpose-built vaults for payment cards, customer records, files up to 5 GB, and customer intake, plus NoPII, a proxy that tokenizes PII in LLM prompts before it reaches the model provider.

This guide compares them feature by feature, with pricing, compliance, live G2 and Capterra reviews, and a verdict for each industry.

Enigma Vault vs Skyflow: Quick Verdict

Enigma Vault is best for protecting cards, records, files, and LLM prompts at a published price, while Skyflow is best for governing sensitive data across a large enterprise.

Enigma Vault: Focused vaults for cards, records, files, and customer intake, plus NoPII for LLM prompts. Every tier is published, each vault has a permanent free tier, and it holds PCI DSS Level 1 and SOC 2 Type II.

Skyflow: One governed enterprise vault with field-level policies, wide data residency, and controls for AI agents and data platforms. It holds PCI DSS Level 1, SOC 2 Type II, and ISO 27001, but publishes no pricing and has two reviews on G2.

I’d point you to Enigma Vault first if you’re a small or mid-size team with a specific exposure to close this quarter:

  • You can be tokenizing real cards or records today on a free tier, without booking a demo.
  • You can budget the whole year from the pricing page: $0, $49.99, or $249.99 a month per vault.
  • Your cards and documents arrive by phone and email, and Enigma Vault covers both channels.
  • Your AI feature sends customer data to OpenAI or Anthropic, and NoPII tokenizes it for $50 a month.

Skyflow’s real strength is governance at scale: one record with different views for every team, policies that follow data into Snowflake and Databricks, and controls over AI agents. It matters most if you’re a large enterprise with many data platforms, many roles, and residency rules outside the US and EU.

  • Choose Enigma Vault if: You need cards, records, documents, or prompt PII protected fast, you want to test before any sales call, or your team has little engineering time.
  • Choose Skyflow if: You govern customer data across a large data estate, you need role-based views of one record, or the vault must run inside your own cloud account.

Bottom Line: If your goal is to get a specific type of sensitive data off your systems, Enigma Vault gets you there faster, at a price you can read, and you can prove it on a free tier first.

The biggest difference is not a feature. It is how each product organizes your data, and it shapes cost, setup time, and who can use it.

To make it concrete, follow one patient booking a procedure. They share a name, date of birth, insurance ID, a card for the deposit, and a scan of their insurance card.

Enigma Vault vs Skyflow: Feature-by-Feature Breakdown

Enigma Vault adds phone capture, 5 GB files, and no-code intake on top of card and PII vaulting, while Skyflow adds SQL on encrypted data and field-level access policies.

Card Tokenization and Payment Routing

Skyflow’s payments vault stores card data under PCI controls and sends it to downstream APIs such as Stripe, Adyen, Plaid, and Visa through its connections. Payments is one of five product lines on its homepage, next to PII, AI agents, healthcare, and residency.

Enigma Vault’s Card Vault was built around how cards reach smaller businesses. Here is what happens when you charge a stored card:

  1. Your app sends a token, never a card number.
  2. The gateway proxy checks the destination against your domain allowlist.
  3. It injects the card into the approved request and sends it to your gateway.
  4. Your app receives the gateway’s response, and the card never lands in your logs.

CVVs are held encrypted for at most 30 minutes, then deleted. Tokens carry 128-bit entropy, and you can choose Luhn-passable tokens if an older system validates card formats.

Neither product offers network tokens or account updater. If those drive your revenue, see our list of the best payment tokenization software.

Phone and Remote Card Capture

Skyflow does not package a phone channel. For Enigma Vault it is a core use case.

With Twilio Pay IVR, callers type their card number on the keypad. The agent never hears it, never types it, and the call recording never captures it.

That matters for travel agencies, clinics, and contact centers, where the most common leak is a card read aloud and written into notes. Our guide to card tokenization software for travel agencies covers this workflow in detail.

Structured PII and Searching Protected Data

Both vaults encrypt individual fields such as Social Security numbers, passport numbers, and bank details. The difference is how you query them afterward.

  • Skyflow: Its G2 profile describes search and SQL analytics on fully encrypted data with fully customizable schemas. Data teams can report on sensitive records without exposing them.
  • Enigma Vault: Data Vault uses AES-256 field encryption with exact-match lookup, so you can find a customer by an encrypted ID without decrypting a table. It accepts batches of up to 5,000 secrets per call for imports and migrations.

If you need to store a field, look it up, and return it to an authorized caller, Data Vault does that with less setup. If you need analytics over protected data, Skyflow is the stronger choice.

Secure File and Document Storage

Skyflow’s Detect API can find and sanitize PII in text, audio, images, and files. It does not offer a governed place to keep the files themselves.

Enigma Vault’s File Vault does:

  • Files up to 5 GB each, under envelope encryption.
  • Presigned URLs for controlled upload and download.
  • Processing status reported over WebSocket.
  • 5 GB a month on the free tier, 150 GB on Plus, and 1 TB on Premium.

One G2 reviewer calls out handling multi-GB files alongside cards and records. Passports, tax returns, medical scans, and contracts get permissions and audit instead of living in shared drives.

Customer Intake for Teams Without Developers

Skyflow protects data once it reaches your systems. It does not replace the channel customers use to send it, and every workflow is built by your engineers.

Enigma Vault’s Customer Vault is a turnkey branded app for front-line staff:

  • Intake Links: Customers submit cards, IDs, and documents with no login.
  • Staff Inbox: Shared, with assignment and e-signatures.
  • Access Controls: Passkeys, enforced MFA, and expiring, password-protected sharing.
  • Isolation: Every tenant gets its own audit trail.

It is designed for teams with no engineering capacity, which describes most agencies, practices, and professional services firms.

Access Control and Audit Trails

Skyflow’s field-level policies are its signature capability. Support sees a masked value, finance the full value, and analytics only a token, all from one record. Nothing in Enigma Vault matches that for organizations with many roles.

Enigma Vault controls access by client and action instead:

  • Scoped OAuth2 machine credentials limit each client to the vault and actions it needs.
  • Domain allowlisting stops tokens being sent to arbitrary destinations.
  • Every customer has its own AES-256 keys, and every request lands in an audit log on every tier.

That model is simpler to reason about and enough for most teams. It is not role-based views of one record.

Data Residency and Deployment Options

Skyflow says its vaults can run almost anywhere in the world, with residency controlled without duplicating infrastructure. Beyond its shared service, it offers a dedicated managed VPC and a Bring Your Own Cloud option in your own AWS or GCP account, and it markets a vault for India’s DPDP Act.

Enigma Vault runs as a managed service on AWS in US and EU regions, across multiple availability zones, with a 99.99% uptime SLA. There is no self-hosted or customer-VPC option.

If your data must stay in a country outside the US and EU, or inside your own cloud account, Skyflow is the one that can do it. For more options, see our list of the best privacy vaults for cross-border data sharing.

How NoPII Works: It replaces recognized PII with consistent tokens, so the same customer maps to the same token across a conversation. It sends the tokenized prompt to the provider and restores real values in the response, including streamed output.

It works with LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, LiteLLM, and Vercel AI SDK. If a request cannot be tokenized, it can be blocked instead of sent raw.

Limits You Should Know:

  • It covers only API routes your app sends through it, not someone pasting data into a chatbot.
  • It does not handle image or audio PII.
  • Detection is not perfect, so test it on representative data.

If you are building one AI feature, NoPII is the faster path. If you are governing an estate of agents and data sources, Skyflow covers more ground. Our list of the best PII tokenization software for LLMs compares more options.

Switching From Skyflow to Enigma Vault: Migration Checklist

To move from Skyflow to Enigma Vault, get export terms in writing, map fields to vaults, involve your acquirer for cards, re-tokenize references, and run both in parallel.

Tokens do not carry over between vaults, so treat any move as a project with its own plan.

  1. Get the Export Terms in Writing: Ask how protected values leave the vault, in what format, and over what channel.
  2. Map Each Skyflow Field to a Vault: Cards to Card Vault, structured fields to Data Vault, documents to File Vault.
  3. Bring Your Acquirer in Early for Cards: Card data moves directly between two PCI-compliant providers, and both must agree the process.
  4. Re-Tokenize Every Stored Reference: This is usually more work than the new integration.
  5. Run Both Vaults Side by Side: Write to both for a few weeks, reconcile, then cut over.

Plan for what does not map one to one. Skyflow’s SQL queries and role-based views have no direct equivalent in Data Vault’s exact-match lookup. Our guide to vault vs vaultless tokenization explains how token formats affect a migration.
