The best TokenEx alternatives for 2026: 1. Enigma Vault, 2. VGS, 3. Evervault, 4. Spreedly, 5. Basis Theory.

TokenEx is the tokenization platform from IXOPAY. TokenEx Vault secures payments, PII and healthcare data, its Universal Tokens work across payment providers, and it supports network tokens. IXOPAY’s IXO One platform combines tokenization with orchestration and payments intelligence (source), and G2 now lists the product as IXOPAY.

The best overall alternative to TokenEx is Enigma Vault, which publishes every plan, starts free and covers cards, personal data and files. Other well-known alternatives to TokenEx are:

  • VGS: Best for payments teams that want the deepest feature set behind a proxy (G2 rating 4.7 from 47 reviews)
  • Evervault: Best for network tokens with per-operation pricing (G2 rating 4.4 from 17 reviews)
  • Spreedly: Best for token ownership and orchestration together (G2 rating 4.4 from 35 reviews)
  • Basis Theory: Best for developer teams that want a PCI AOC included

We picked these five from the search itself: VGS and Evervault advertise on it, IXOPAY’s own comparison post names VGS and Spreedly, Basis Theory publishes an IXOPAY alternative page, and GetApp lists Enigma Vault and Basis Theory. Payment gateways such as Stripe, Adyen and Braintree also appear in this search, but they process payments alongside a vault rather than replace one.

Enigma Vault Card Vault is a credit card tokenization API with a payment gateway proxy. It tokenizes cards the moment they are captured and charges them through the gateway you already use, so raw card data never touches your systems. Data Vault does the same for personal data with searchable field-level encryption, and File Vault stores sensitive files behind short-lived URLs. For a TokenEx buyer, the change is how you start and what you pay: Every plan is published, each vault starts with a free sandbox, and paid plans start at $49 a month. The trade is payments depth, because Enigma Vault does not list network tokens, Account Updater or payment orchestration.

  • Beyond cards: Searchable field-level encryption in Data Vault and encrypted file storage in File Vault

How to Choose a TokenEx Alternative

Is your reason price or scope? Price points to Enigma Vault at $49 or Spreedly at $750. Wanting more payments features points to VGS or Evervault.

Do you rely on network tokens or Account Updater? VGS, Evervault and Spreedly offer them; Enigma Vault does not. List what you use before assuming parity.

Is your problem still only cards? If personal data or documents are now in scope, Enigma Vault covers them at the same published prices.

Can you absorb a re-tokenization project? Every option means new tokens in every system that holds one. If that is not possible this quarter, renegotiating with IXOPAY may be the honest answer.

What Migrating Off TokenEx Actually Involves

  • Confirm the export path first: Get in writing how IXOPAY exports stored card data, in what format and over what channel, while you are still a customer.
  • Expect a PCI-to-PCI transfer: Card data moves between two compliant environments, and both providers must agree the process. Involve your acquirer early.
  • Your tokens do not travel: Universal Tokens are TokenEx tokens. Every system that stores one needs updating to the new vendor’s token.
  • Check network token continuity: If you use network tokens, ask how they are re-provisioned with the new vendor.
  • Dual-run before cutting over: Write to both vaults and reconcile for a month or two before switching off TokenEx.
