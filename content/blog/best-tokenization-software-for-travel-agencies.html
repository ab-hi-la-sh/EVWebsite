Comparing the best tokenization software for travel agencies of 2026: 1. Enigma Vault, 2. PCI Booking, 3. VGS, 4. PCI Proxy, 5. Spreedly, 6. IXOPAY, and 7. Basis Theory.

About the author: I’m Dr. Tony Alex, Chief Executive Officer of Enigma Vault, where I lead our work in encryption, tokenization, and sensitive-data protection. I have more than two decades of experience building enterprise software businesses.

In 1999 I founded US eDirect to develop software for parks, campgrounds, and recreation agencies, bringing reservations and payments into one cloud system, and led it through its acquisition by Tyler Technologies in 2022.

Disclosure: Enigma Vault publishes this guide and appears first in it. Every platform here is judged on the same criteria, and the review ratings come from G2 and Capterra.

Tokenization software for travel agencies replaces a traveler’s card number and passport details with tokens that your GDS, mid-office, and advisors can store and use, while the real values stay in a separate vault. It protects cards from the phone desk and booking link through the PNR, the supplier payment, and the invoice.

The best options in 2026 are Enigma Vault for agency desks that take cards by phone, link, and web; PCI Booking and Spreedly for platforms passing cards to many suppliers; VGS for OTAs filling supplier web pages; PCI Proxy for airline and travel sellers; IXOPAY for high-volume sellers routing across acquirers; and Basis Theory for OTAs routing cards to GDS partners and suppliers.

To choose, list where traveler cards reach your agency in a normal week, then pick the platform that covers your riskiest channel and pays suppliers the way you already do.

Cards and Passports in GDS PNRs and NDC Orders

A PNR in Sabre, Amadeus, or Travelport carries a form of payment, passport details, loyalty numbers, and free-text remarks. Apart from PCI Booking’s inbound GDS profiles, no platform here publishes a GDS connector, so placement is the rule: token in the CRM, card released only at the GDS step.

  • Remarks and queues: Never store card numbers or CVVs in free text.
  • Passport data: CBP’s APIS program requires passenger data for commercial flights into and out of the US; collect passports through secure intake.
  • NDC orders: Apply the same rule: keep the token, and release the card only at the order step.
  • CVV handling: CVV must not be kept after authorization; Card Vault deletes it within 30 minutes.
