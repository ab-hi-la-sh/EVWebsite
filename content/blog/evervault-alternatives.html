The best Evervault alternatives for 2026: 1. Enigma Vault, 2. VGS, 3. Basis Theory, 4. Spreedly, 5. TokenEx / IXOPAY, 6. Skyflow, 7. PCI Proxy.

Teams evaluating Evervault alternatives usually think the choice is between holding card data and not holding it. That is not the choice. Every option here keeps raw card numbers out of your systems. The real question is narrower: Where does the data live, and what does it cost you to use it again?

Evervault answers that with dual custody. You hold encrypted cards without the keys, Evervault holds keys without your cards, and you pay per operation on top of a monthly base. It is a clean model with unusually transparent pricing.

If you are here, you are likely weighing whether a four-figure base plus metered decrypts, and the job of storing ciphertext yourself, still make sense for a workload that reads card data often or has grown past cards.

Why Teams Look For Evervault Alternatives

Evervault earns its place. Dual custody is a genuinely different security posture, Enclaves on AWS Nitro are real engineering, and it publishes per-operation rates almost nobody else in this market discloses. The reasons buyers evaluate alternatives are about cost shape, custody and scope, not the quality of the encryption.

  • The base plus usage adds up: Platform is $995 a month before any usage, recommended for under 25,000 transactions a month. Encrypts, relays and functions are unlimited, but decrypts are $0.005 each, file decrypts $0.02 and fingerprints $0.005, so a read-heavy workload pays twice.
  • You store the ciphertext: Evervault returns encrypted card data for you to hold in your own systems while the key stays with Evervault. Basis Theory, which competes directly for this keyword, characterises that as leaving more operational burden with your team. That is a competitor's framing, but the architectural difference is real and worth understanding before you choose.
  • It is cards-first: Evervault's centre of gravity is payments, so if structured PII, documents or customer intake have entered your scope, much of the platform goes unused.

If those points match your evaluation, the alternatives below sort cleanly by how they answer them.

How We Evaluated These Evervault Alternatives

We scored every provider on the same six criteria, weighted for a team already running Evervault and deciding whether to move. Weights sum to 100%, and the Overall score in the table is the weighted result.

  • Payments depth (20%): Network tokens, Account Updater, 3DS, BIN lookup, card-present.
  • Custody model clarity (15%): Who holds the card, who holds the key, and what your systems end up storing.
  • Processor independence (15%): Do tokens move between gateways, or lock to one?
  • Coverage beyond cards (15%): Structured PII, files and intake.
  • Compliance evidence (10%): What each vendor publishes on its own trust pages.

How To Choose An Evervault Alternative

Is your reason cost or architecture? Cost points to Enigma Vault at $49.99 or Spreedly at $750. Architecture points to Basis Theory, where the vault holds the card instead of handing back ciphertext.

How read-heavy is your workload? Evervault's unlimited encrypts with metered decrypts suits write-once, read-rarely. If you read constantly, a flat-tier vault is usually cheaper and easier to forecast.

Which payments features do you actually use? Account Updater, network tokens and BIN lookup are not uniform across this list. Inventory them before assuming parity.

Has your scope widened past cards? Documents, structured PII or customer intake point to Enigma Vault or Skyflow rather than any payments-only vault.

Where must your data live? Enigma Vault runs US and EU. Basis Theory's entry plan is US-only. Confirm residency before shortlisting.

Decrypt everything first, deliberately: Your stored ciphertext is useless without Evervault's keys. Agree the bulk export and decryption path in writing before you sign with anyone new, and budget for the decrypt charges that export will incur.

Expect a PCI-to-PCI transfer: Card data moves between two compliant environments and both providers must agree the process. Bring your acquirer in early.

Your tokens do not travel: New vault, new tokens. Every system holding a reference needs updating.

Enclave logic does not port: If you run computation on encrypted data inside Evervault, no other vendor here offers a direct equivalent. Plan where that logic moves.

Dual-run before cutting over: Write to both and reconcile. Budget for two vendors for a month or two.
