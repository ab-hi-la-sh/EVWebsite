The best Protegrity alternatives for 2026: 1. Enigma Vault, 2. Fortanix, 3. OpenText Voltage SecureData, 4. Thales CipherTrust Tokenization, 5. comforte TAMUNIO, 6. Skyflow, 7. Baffle, 8. Capital One Databolt.

Protegrity is an enterprise data protection platform that tokenizes, masks and encrypts sensitive data across databases, data warehouses and AI workflows, and its newer AI editions add discovery, semantic guardrails and agent controls. G2 lists it under categories including Data Masking, Data De-Identification, Encryption and Sensitive Data Discovery. Buyers usually compare it with other options on three things: How tokens are made (vaulted or vaultless), which platforms it protects natively, and whether they can see a price before a sales call.

The best overall alternative to Protegrity is Enigma Vault, which publishes every plan, starts free, and covers both application data (Data Vault) and LLM traffic (Enigma Vault NoPII) on one PCI DSS Level 1 and SOC 2 Type II vault. Other well-known alternatives to Protegrity are:

  • Fortanix: Best suited to vaultless, format-preserving tokenization with HSM-backed keys (G2 rating 4.5 from 2 reviews)
  • OpenText Voltage SecureData: Offers format-preserving encryption across mainframe, Hadoop, Teradata and Snowflake
  • Thales CipherTrust Tokenization: Known for vaulted and vaultless tokenization in one platform (G2 rating 4.0 from 6 reviews)
  • Baffle: Best for protecting existing databases without code changes (G2 rating 4.4 from 11 reviews)

Every rating in this guide links to the product’s G2 or Capterra page, so you can read the verified reviews behind each like, dislike and score for yourself.

Why Teams Look for Protegrity Alternatives

Protegrity has real strengths: Vaultless tokenization that scales, native protectors for Databricks, Snowflake, Redshift and Cloudera, and AI editions with guardrails and agent controls. These are the reasons buyers most often look elsewhere.

  • Pricing needs a sales call: Protegrity publishes no prices; its pricing page returns a not-found page, and the Team and Enterprise editions start with a demo.
  • Deployment is AWS-first for now: The Enterprise edition page says current deployment is AWS-specific, on Kubernetes (EKS, ECS or Docker Compose).
  • It is built for large estates: Policy management, protectors for each platform family and a Kubernetes deployment are a lot to run when the job is protecting a few fields or one AI feature.
  • Reviewers mention price: One G2 reviewer finds the price range a little high, and small-business reviewers rate it lower than enterprise ones.

How We Scored These Protegrity Alternatives

We scored every provider on the same six criteria, weighted for a team replacing or avoiding an enterprise data protection platform. Weights sum to 100%, and the score in the table is the weighted result.

  • Pricing transparency and time to start (25%): Whether you can see a price and protect data without a sales cycle.
  • Tokenization approach (20%): Vaulted or vaultless, format preservation, and whether data stays usable.
  • AI data protection (15%): Coverage of prompts, model responses, RAG and agents.
  • Deployment options (15%): Cloud, on-premises, hybrid and data residency.
  • Platform coverage (15%): Native support for databases, warehouses and big data platforms.
  • Compliance evidence (10%): What the vendor publishes on its own trust pages.

This is an editorial comparison published by one of the vendors in it. Weight platform coverage higher and OpenText Voltage SecureData leads; weight compliance evidence higher and Skyflow climbs.

Enigma Vault is a data security platform built on a PCI DSS Level 1 and SOC 2 Type II vault. Its Data Vault is a searchable field-level encryption API: It encrypts personal data, secrets and identifiers with AES-256, your database holds tokens, and exact-match lookups on encrypted values keep working.

For AI workloads, Enigma Vault NoPII is an LLM privacy proxy that tokenizes sensitive values before they reach the model provider and restores them in the response. It covers prompts, responses, tool-call arguments and results, and the embeddings endpoint.

For a Protegrity buyer, the difference is how you start. Every Enigma Vault plan is published, both products start free, and you protect your first field or prompt through an API or a base URL change rather than a platform rollout. The trade is reach: Protegrity ships native protectors for Snowflake, Databricks, Redshift and Cloudera, which Enigma Vault does not.

Key features

  • Searchable field-level encryption: AES-256 encryption with exact-match lookup on encrypted values
  • Batch tokenization: Up to 500 values per call, or 5,000 on Growth and Business
  • LLM privacy proxy: Enigma Vault NoPII tokenizes PII before it reaches the model provider and restores it in the response
  • AI coverage: Prompts, responses, tool-call arguments and results, and embeddings for RAG pipelines
  • Detection: 11 entity types on by default, 25+ more to opt into, and 14 families of secrets and API keys
  • Fail-safe by default: If NoPII cannot tokenize a request, it blocks it rather than forwarding it
  • Deployment options: Cloud API, with a self-hosted NoPII edition on Enterprise
  • Compliance documents: PCI AOC, SOC 2 Type II report and DPA on paid Data Vault plans; BAA for healthcare on Enterprise

How to Choose a Protegrity Alternative

Answer these questions in order. Each one narrows the list.

1. Is most of your sensitive data in a warehouse or lakehouse? Look at Capital One Databolt for Snowflake and Databricks, or OpenText Voltage SecureData for Teradata and Hadoop.

2. Is it in your own applications and databases? Look at Enigma Vault’s Data Vault, an API that encrypts fields and still lets you search them, or Baffle, which protects databases without code changes.

3. Is it flowing into LLMs and AI agents? Look at Enigma Vault NoPII, a privacy proxy that tokenizes customer details before they reach the model and puts them back in the answer, or Skyflow for a wider vault program.

4. Do you need vaultless tokens? Choose Fortanix, OpenText Voltage SecureData or Thales CipherTrust.

5. Do you run mainframe or HPE NonStop systems? Look at comforte TAMUNIO or OpenText Voltage SecureData.

6. How quickly do you need a price? Enigma Vault publishes every plan, and Fortanix offers a free trial. The others start with sales.
