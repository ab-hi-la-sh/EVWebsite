Our mission.

Enterprises should not have to choose between AI capability and data privacy. Enigma Vault exists to remove that trade-off, so every organization can harness the full power of generative AI without the fear of a false answer or the risk of exposing the data underneath.

Answers you can prove.Data you never expose.

AI moved faster than anyone's ability to trust it.

Adoption outran trust on every front at once. Answers shipped that no one could verify. Prompts carried customer data and secrets straight to model providers. And the data underneath, the cards, files, and records, was exposed to every new system that touched it.

Everyone was shipping AI. Few could prove it was safe.

That’s why we built Enigma Vault: to verify the output, protect the input, and secure the data underneath. Because trust you can’t prove isn’t trust.

Built by the people who needed it most.

Entrepreneurs, inventors, CISOs, and AI experts, with decades of combined experience building AI products and securing enterprise data, and just as long feeling the pain.

This is not a team that read about the problem. We have built AI products that hallucinated in front of customers. We have sat in the audit and answered for the data. We have filed the patents and shipped the infrastructure. Enigma Vault is the product we wished existed every one of those times.

  • Inventors. Patent claims filed across 18 provisional families, Patent Pending.
  • Security leadership. CISO experience and a platform that is a PCI DSS Level 1 Service Provider with SOC 2 Type II attestation.
  • AI builders. Products in production across 9+ model providers, with the scars to show for it.

What we believe.

Trust is infrastructure, not a promise.

Privacy and reliability have to be enforced outside the application and outside the model, where they hold even when either one gets it wrong.

Refusal is a feature.

A system that declines to guess, and says so in writing, is worth more than one that always answers.

The safest data is the data you never hold.

Custody, not encryption alone, decides your breach story.

If we can’t prove it, we don’t claim it.

Every claim we make is checkable: in the output, in the audit trail, by your auditor.

Don’t take our word for it. Check it.
