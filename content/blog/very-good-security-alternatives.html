The best Very Good Security alternatives for 2026: 1. Enigma Vault, 2. Basis Theory, 3. Spreedly, 4. Evervault, 5. TokenEx / IXOPAY, 6. PCI Proxy, 7. Skyflow.

Most comparisons of VGS argue about features. The decision is usually made one layer down, on architecture: Do you want a proxy sitting in your payment path, or do you want to call a vault directly?

VGS answers that one way. Its platform aliases sensitive data through a reverse proxy before it reaches your systems, then re-inserts the real value in real time on the way out. That design is why teams with a working payment flow pick VGS, and it is also why some of them leave.

If you are here, you are likely weighing whether an inline proxy in your payment path, and a four-figure floor, are still the right trade for your volume, your latency budget and the data you now need to protect.

Why Teams Look For Very Good Security Alternatives

VGS earns its place. Processor-agnostic aliasing, network tokens, Account Updater, 3DS and wallet payments add up to the deepest payments feature set in this comparison. The reasons buyers evaluate alternatives are about price, architecture and scope, not the depth of the platform.

  • The price floor: VGS publishes plans starting at $1,000 a month. There is a free tier to begin, but the first real step is the highest published entry price in this market, and for a team tokenizing modest volume that is a lot of fixed cost before anything else happens.
  • The proxy is a dependency: Routing payment traffic through an external proxy adds a hop and a vendor your checkout now depends on. For many teams that trade is worth it. For teams with strict latency budgets or unusual message-signing requirements, it is the reason to look elsewhere.
  • It is payments-weighted: If your problem turned out to be customer documents, structured PII or intake, much of that payments breadth goes unused while you keep paying for it.

If those points match your evaluation, the alternatives below sort cleanly by how they answer them.

How We Evaluated These VGS Alternatives

We scored every provider on the same six criteria, weighted for a team already running VGS and deciding whether to move. Weights sum to 100%, and the Overall score in the table is the weighted result.

  • Entry cost and pricing transparency (25%): What the first real tier costs, and whether it is published.
  • Architecture fit (20%): Proxy, direct vault API, or encryption-based, and what that does to your request path.
  • Payments depth (20%): Network tokens, Account Updater, 3DS, BIN lookup, card-present.
  • Processor independence (15%): Do tokens move between gateways, or lock to one?
  • Compliance evidence (10%): What the vendor publishes on its own trust pages.
  • Coverage beyond cards (10%): Structured PII, files and intake.

This is an editorial comparison published by one of the vendors in it. Re-weight it and the order moves: Drop payments depth and raise coverage, and Enigma Vault and Skyflow climb.

On review scores: G2 and Capterra ratings here were checked on each product's page on 16 October 2026, and every one is reported with its review count. An average drawn from six reviews and an average drawn from forty-seven are not the same claim.

Where a vendor has no published reviews, we say so plainly rather than filling the gap with something softer.

On PCI scope: Moving between two PCI DSS Level 1 providers does not by itself widen or narrow your scope. Scope is determined by your environment and integration, and your acquirer or a QSA confirms it.

How To Choose A VGS Alternative

Is your reason architectural or financial? Architectural points to Basis Theory, a direct API. Financial points to Enigma Vault at $49.99 or Spreedly at $750. Wanting to keep the proxy but change vendor points to PCI Proxy.

Which payments features do you actually use? Inventory them first. Account Updater, network tokens and BIN lookup are not uniform across this list, and assuming parity is how migrations go wrong.

Is your problem still cards? If documents, structured PII or intake have entered scope, Enigma Vault or Skyflow serves you better than any payments-only vault.

Where must your data live? Enigma Vault runs US and EU. Basis Theory's entry plan is US-only.

Can you absorb a re-tokenization project? Every option here means new tokens and updating every system that holds one. If you cannot take that on this quarter, the honest answer may be to stay and renegotiate.

What Migrating Off VGS Actually Involves

Confirm the export path first: Get in writing how card data is exported, in what format and over what channel, while you are still a customer in good standing.

Expect a PCI-to-PCI transfer: Card data moves between two compliant environments and both providers must agree the process. Involve your acquirer early rather than at cut-over.

Your tokens do not travel: New vault, new tokens. Every system holding a VGS alias needs updating, and that is usually more work than the new integration.

Unwind the proxy carefully: If VGS routes your traffic, removing it means your application starts handling calls it has not handled before. Test the failure modes, not just the happy path.

Dual-run before cutting over: Write to both vaults and reconcile. Budget for two vendors for a month or two.

Check what you lose: Account Updater, network token provisioning and BIN lookup are not uniform across this list. Inventory what you use before assuming parity.
