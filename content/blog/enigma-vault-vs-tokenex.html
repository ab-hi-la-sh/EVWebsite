If TokenEx and Enigma Vault are both on your shortlist, you are probably deciding between a long-established enterprise payments platform and a vault you can price and start on today.

TokenEx has been in payment tokenization for more than a decade. Since merging with IXOPAY in April 2024, it sits inside a platform that connects merchants to more than 200 payment service providers, whereas Enigma Vault is a PCI DSS Level 1 tokenization platform where Card Vault sits alongside Data Vault for customer records, File Vault for documents up to 5 GB, Customer Vault for intake, and NoPII for LLM prompts.

This guide compares the two on features, card channels, pricing, compliance, live G2 and Capterra reviews, and the best fit for each industry.

Enigma Vault vs TokenEx: Quick Verdict

Enigma Vault: Focused vaults with published pricing, phone card capture, document storage, a no-code intake app, and an LLM privacy proxy. It starts free and tops out at $499 a month per product before Enterprise.

TokenEx: An enterprise payments tokenization platform with Universal Tokens, in-store P2PE, network tokens, and orchestration through IXOPAY. IXOPAY and Capterra list plans from $1,000 a month, with exact pricing on a quote.

I’d point you to Enigma Vault first if you’re a business whose cards and documents arrive through people, not terminals:

  • You can price and test it on real data without a sales cycle.
  • Cards reach you by phone or email, and Twilio Pay and Customer Vault cover both.
  • You need to protect customer records, files, or LLM prompts as well as cards.
  • Your team has little engineering time for a platform rollout.

TokenEx’s real strength is enterprise payments breadth. It matters most if you’re a retailer or travel brand running stores and ecommerce across several processors and countries, with an approval-rate target to hit.

  • Choose Enigma Vault if: You want a free tier, published pricing, and coverage for phone, email, documents, and prompts.
  • Choose TokenEx if: You need card-present encryption, multi-processor routing, network tokens, and payments analytics from one vendor.

Bottom Line: TokenEx fits merchants who need a payments platform. Enigma Vault fits businesses that need sensitive data out of their inboxes, phone lines, and databases this month.

How Enigma Vault and TokenEx Capabilities Compare

Universal Tokens and Processor Independence

TokenEx built its reputation on Universal Tokens, a token layer not tied to any one payment processor. You can move volume between providers without collecting cards again.

Inside IXOPAY, those tokens now sit under orchestration that connects to more than 200 payment service providers and 300 payment methods, with routing, risk, and reconciliation on top.

Enigma Vault takes the same stance on lock-in with a lighter footprint. Card Vault stores the card, and the gateway proxy charges through the REST gateway you already use (Stripe, Braintree, PayPal, Authorize.net, and others). Switching processors later is a configuration change, but Enigma Vault does not route or orchestrate for you.

Proxying Card Data to Third Parties

TokenEx’s Transparent Gateway API sits between your systems and third-party endpoints and detokenizes on the way out. A G2 reviewer credits the iFrame plus transparent gateway with reducing their PCI scope because their own system never touches card data.

Enigma Vault’s gateway proxy does the same job for payment gateways, with strict defaults:

  • Domain allowlisting, so tokens cannot be forwarded to arbitrary destinations.
  • Scoped OAuth2 credentials per client and action.
  • No card number in your logs.

For straightforward gateway charging the two are close. TokenEx covers more kinds of downstream endpoints.

Network Tokens, Account Updater, and Payments Intelligence

IXOPAY packages a deep payments toolkit around TokenEx:

  • Network tokens, account updater, 3DS, BIN lookup, and PAR.
  • Acquirer PCI tools.
  • Analytics, anomaly detection, and monitoring.
  • IXONav, an AI assistant for payments operations.

For an enterprise merchant tuning approval rates across regions, that is a serious toolkit. Enigma Vault offers none of these. It protects and forwards the card, and your gateway handles authorization and 3DS.

Card Capture by Phone

Phone payments are where card numbers most often leak: read aloud, written in notes, and caught on call recordings.

Enigma Vault’s Twilio Pay IVR lets callers enter the card on their keypad so agents never hear or type it. Card Vault then holds the CVV encrypted for at most 30 minutes before deleting it. TokenEx does not package a phone channel.

Customer Records, Health Data, and Files

TokenEx’s Capterra listing describes tokenizing PCI, PII, PHI, and even unstructured data, and integrating with nearly any processor. In practice its IXOPAY packages are organized around payments, so non-card data is something you design yourself on the API.

Enigma Vault sells each case as its own product with a free tier:

Customer Intake Without Developers

TokenEx is a developer and payments-operations platform with no front-line app for collecting information from customers.

Enigma Vault’s Customer Vault is a turnkey branded application. It includes no-login intake links, a staff inbox with assignment, e-signatures, passkeys and enforced MFA, expiring password-protected sharing, and a per-tenant audit trail. It replaces email and attachments for travel agencies, clinics, accounting firms, and law firms.

AI: Agents That Pay vs Prompts That Carry PII

IXOPAY’s Agentic Suite includes a Payment Agent, Universal Tokens for agent transactions, and an MCP server, all aimed at AI agents that initiate payments.

Enigma Vault’s NoPII addresses a different AI risk: customer PII inside LLM prompts. It replaces recognized PII with consistent tokens before the prompt reaches the model provider and restores real values in the response, including streamed output.

It works across nine providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq, Together, and Fireworks). It covers only routes your app sends through it, so test detection on your own data. Starter is $49 a month for 50M tokens.

Switching From TokenEx to Enigma Vault

  1. Agree the Card Migration: Cards move directly between two PCI-compliant providers, and both vendors and your acquirer need to agree the process.
  2. Check Token Formats: If downstream systems depend on a TokenEx token scheme, decide which Enigma Vault format (random or Luhn-passable) fits.
  3. Plan for P2PE and Orchestration First: Enigma Vault does not provide them, so settle in-store payments and routing before moving cards.
  4. Re-Tokenize and Run Both for a Few Weeks: Update every stored reference, reconcile, then cut over.
