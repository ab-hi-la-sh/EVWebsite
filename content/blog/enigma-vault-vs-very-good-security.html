If you are comparing Enigma Vault and Very Good Security (VGS), you want card numbers and personal data off your servers, and you are deciding how much platform you need to get there.

VGS built its name on a reverse proxy that swaps sensitive data for aliases before it reaches your servers, and in 2026 it calls itself “the world’s leader in payment tokenization,” whereas Enigma Vault is a PCI DSS Level 1 vault platform your app calls directly, with separate vaults for payment cards, customer records, files up to 5 GB, and customer intake, and a gateway proxy used only at the moment you charge a card.

This guide compares the two on architecture, features, pricing, compliance support, and live reviews on G2 and Capterra, with a recommendation for each industry.

Enigma Vault vs VGS: Quick Verdict

Enigma Vault is best for low-cost PCI scope reduction across cards, records, files, and prompts, while VGS is best for network tokens, card issuing, and bundled audit help.

Enigma Vault: A direct-API vault for cards, records, files, customer intake, and LLM prompts. It starts free, every tier is published up to $249.99 a month per vault, and it holds PCI DSS Level 1 and SOC 2 Type II.

VGS: A proxy-first payments security platform with network tokens, account updater, card management, and compliance services. It starts at $1,000 a month, and G2 users report typical contracts of $19,000 to $108,000 a year.

I’d point you to Enigma Vault first if you’re a startup or mid-size business that needs PCI scope reduction without a five-figure contract:

  • You can run Card Vault Premium for a full year for about $3,000, against VGS’s $12,000 annual floor.
  • You protect more than cards: customer records, documents, and prompts.
  • Your cards reach you by phone, and Twilio Pay keeps them off the call.
  • You would rather not have a third-party proxy sitting in front of all your traffic.

VGS’s real strength is payments depth plus easy retrofitting. It matters most if you’re a fintech or marketplace where card lifecycle and authorization rates drive revenue, or you have dozens of existing endpoints you would rather proxy than rewrite.

  • Choose Enigma Vault if: You want a published price, broad data coverage, and nothing in your request path.
  • Choose VGS if: You need network tokens, card issuing, or bundled PCI audit help, and the budget fits.

Bottom Line: VGS is a strong payments platform priced for fintechs. If your goal is to stop holding sensitive data, Enigma Vault does that from $0.

Proxy-Based vs API-Based Tokenization: How Enigma Vault and VGS Differ

Enigma Vault is called directly by your app and proxies only at the moment you charge, whereas VGS proxies your traffic and swaps data for aliases before it reaches your servers.

This architectural choice shapes everything else, including how much code you change, who sits in your traffic, and what you maintain over time.

QuestionEnigma Vault (API-based)VGS (proxy-based)
How data is protected on the way inYour app calls the vault and stores the tokenThe inbound proxy swaps values for aliases before your app sees them
How data reaches your processorGateway proxy injects the card at charge timeOutbound proxy restores values on configured routes
Who sits in your request pathNobody, except at charge timeVGS, on every proxied route
Code changesA handful of explicit API callsLittle application code; route configuration instead
Ongoing maintenanceAllowlist and credentialsRoutes, matching rules, and promotion between vaults

Where the Proxy Model Helps: You can protect existing endpoints with little code, and reviewers on G2 repeatedly say they understood the proxy quickly.

Where It Costs You: VGS reviewers mention reloading routes for changes to take effect, needing support for complex matching, and promoting routes between vaults by hand in YAML.

Where the API Model Helps: All your other traffic stays on your own infrastructure, and developers can try every call first in the live Swagger UI at api.enigmavault.io.

If you have dozens of existing endpoints and little appetite for code changes, the proxy model saves time. If you would rather keep third parties out of your request path, the direct vault is cleaner.

How Enigma Vault and VGS Protect Cards, PII, Files, and AI Data

Enigma Vault covers phone capture, a BAA, 5 GB file storage, no-code intake, and prompt PII, while VGS offers richer checkout components and card payments tooling.

Card Capture Online and in Apps

VGS Collect provides iframe-based form fields for web checkouts, with mobile SDKs and wallet support alongside. It is a mature, widely used component.

One VGS reviewer on G2 wishes they could pre-fill certain Collect fields without running two parallel forms. Enigma Vault covers the same ground with hosted forms and its API, but for a self-serve checkout on web and mobile, VGS gives you more ready-made pieces.

Card Capture by Phone

An inbound proxy protects data arriving over HTTP. It does nothing for a card number read aloud on a call and written into a ticket.

Enigma Vault’s Twilio Pay IVR closes that gap. Callers enter their card on the keypad, and agents never hear or type it. For travel agencies, hotels, clinics, and contact centers, this is often the biggest source of card exposure.

Network Tokens, Account Updater, and Card Data Services

This is VGS’s strongest ground. Its payments line includes:

  • A card management platform for issuing.
  • Network tokens and account updater.
  • Card attributes, BIN lookup, and account validation.
  • 3DS, wallet payments, and tokenized account numbers.

In a VGS testimonial, SumUp credits it with saving nearly $1M and six months of development. For a fintech optimizing approval rates or a platform issuing cards, that toolkit is hard to match.

Enigma Vault charges through the REST gateway you already use (Stripe, Braintree, PayPal, Authorize.net, and others), and switching processors later is a configuration change. It does not offer network tokens, account updater, BIN lookup, or card issuing.

Personal and Health Data

VGS protects PII alongside cards. Its public pages do not mention a HIPAA BAA.

Enigma Vault’s Data Vault encrypts fields such as SSNs, passport numbers, and bank details with AES-256, supports exact-match lookup on encrypted values, and handles batches of up to 5,000 secrets. A BAA is available.

One VGS reviewer says cost pushed them to use VGS only for their most sensitive data and encrypt names and emails themselves. Data Vault’s free and $49.99 tiers are priced for that kind of everyday PII.

Files and Documents

The two treat files differently:

  • VGS: Handles file-based flows through SFTP and offers large file transfers in its Growth package. A G2 reviewer notes the SFTP product is less mature than the HTTP proxy.
  • Enigma Vault: File Vault is a governed store rather than a transfer channel, with files up to 5 GB each under envelope encryption, presigned URLs, and status over WebSocket, from 5 GB a month on the free tier.

If you need somewhere safe to keep passports, tax returns, or scans, File Vault is built for it.

Customer Intake for Non-Technical Teams

Several VGS reviewers say the platform is hard for less technical people to use; it is built for engineers.

Enigma Vault’s Customer Vault is a turnkey branded app for front-line staff. It includes no-login intake links, a shared inbox with assignment, e-signatures, passkeys and enforced MFA, expiring password-protected sharing, and a per-tenant audit trail.

Switching From VGS to Enigma Vault

To move from VGS to Enigma Vault, inventory your routes, agree the card migration with your acquirer, re-tokenize aliases, replace payments add-ons, and run both.

  1. Inventory Your Routes: List every inbound and outbound VGS route and the fields each one aliases. Each becomes a vault call or a gateway proxy request.
  2. Agree the Card Migration: Card data moves directly between two PCI-compliant environments, and both providers and your acquirer must agree the process.
  3. Re-Tokenize Stored Aliases: VGS aliases will not work in a new vault, so every table holding one needs a mapping pass.
  4. Replace Payments Add-Ons: If you rely on VGS network tokens or account updater, check whether your gateway provides them.
  5. Run Both, Then Cut Over: Write new data to both for a few weeks and reconcile. Your gateway relationship does not change.
