HIPAA De-Identification: What Tokenization Does and Does Not Do

The Privacy Rule at 45 CFR 164.514(b) offers two routes to de-identified data: Safe Harbor, which removes 18 identifier types, and Expert Determination by a qualified expert.

If a staff member removes the name, date of birth, and MRN before pasting a note into AI, is it still PHI? Usually yes. Those are three of the 18 Safe Harbor identifiers, so dates of service, ZIP codes, phone numbers, and other details left in the note can keep it PHI.

Reversible tokens are pseudonymization. While a vault or key can restore the name, the data generally stays PHI; tokenization narrows access without de-identifying a data set on its own.
