Protecto and Skyflow are both data protection platforms that keep names, account numbers, clinical notes, and credentials out of what your AI systems see. Protecto here is the AI data security company at protecto.ai, not Protegrity.

Protecto is best for teams that want masking and unmasking inside AI pipelines, with published plans from $250 a month, while Skyflow is best for enterprises that need a governed data privacy vault with runtime control for AI agents. This guide compares Protecto and Skyflow on architecture, features, pricing, compliance, and reviews, with NoPII, Enigma Vault’s LLM privacy proxy, alongside as a free-to-start third option.

Protecto vs Skyflow: Quick Verdict

Protecto: An AI-focused masking platform with a headless REST API for mask and unmask calls, a privacy gateway for chat tools, a LiteLLM adapter, and deployment as multi-tenant SaaS or on-premises, including air-gapped. Where Protecto and Skyflow Differ in Practice

How Each Protects LLM Data

Protecto masks with typed, context-preserving tokens, with format-preserving and entropy-based options, and returns real values only through explicit, permissioned unmask calls. A gateway for chat tools maps Active Directory or Okta groups to a per-user key. Skyflow’s Detect de-identifies text, files, audio, and images, offers vault tokens, entity counters, and entity-only tokens, and re-identifies by role.

NoPII

NoPII tokenizes inside the proxy, keeps the same token for the same customer across a conversation, and restores real values in the response. Real values are revealed only to the end user who created the token, under a time-boxed grant, or to a server key with an opt-in scope, and every reveal attempt is logged.

Detection Coverage and Accuracy Claims

Protecto’s current product pages say 50+ built-in entity types plus custom entities, and its docs list 53. An older page still says 200+. It reports a 99.74% F1 score on an April 2026 internal test of 75 cases, and 99% recall and 96% precision in a four-week proof of concept at Bank Muscat. Those are vendor-reported figures. The benchmark it cites, hosted on its own site, compares it with AWS Comprehend and Microsoft Presidio, not Skyflow.

Skyflow documents 60 entity types and more than 40 languages and makes no accuracy claims. No independent head-to-head exists, so run both on your own data.

Deployment: Your Network or Theirs

Protecto offers multi-tenant SaaS and an on-premises or private-cloud model on AWS, GCP, Azure, or your own data center, with air-gapped listed as a use. In that model you own scaling, monitoring, backups, upgrades, and availability, setup takes days to weeks, and the trial is SaaS only.

Skyflow offers multi-tenant SaaS, Virtual Private Skyflow in a dedicated VPC, and BYOC on AWS or GCP, where Skyflow operates the service in your account. We found no customer-operated on-premises option in its docs. NoPII is a managed service, and its self-hosted edition runs under an annual Enterprise license with no token meter.

Policy, Governance, and What Is Still on the Roadmap

Protecto’s docs list context-based access control, GPTGuard MCP, and Dynamic Auth as coming soon or in preview, while its marketing pages present context-based access control as available.
