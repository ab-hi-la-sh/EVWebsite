If you are weighing Enigma Vault against Basis Theory, you already know you want card data out of your systems. The real question is what you need to do with that data once it is vaulted.

Basis Theory is a programmable payments vault for engineering teams, with network tokens, account updater, smart routing, custom code on vaulted data, and agentic payments, while Enigma Vault is a PCI DSS Level 1 tokenization platform that protects payment cards, customer records, documents up to 5 GB, customer intake, and LLM prompts, including sensitive data that never touches a checkout.

This guide compares the two on features, cost at three business sizes, compliance, G2 and Capterra reviews, and fit by industry.

Enigma Vault vs Basis Theory: Quick Verdict

Enigma Vault: Focused vaults for cards, records, and files, plus Customer Vault for intake and NoPII for LLM prompts. It starts free, tops out at $499 a month per product before Enterprise, and offers US and EU hosting on every plan.

Basis Theory: A programmable payments vault with network tokens, account updater, 3DS, smart routing, Reactors, and agentic payments. It starts at $995 a month, its entry plan is US-only, and it has no reviews yet on G2 or Capterra.

I’d point you to Enigma Vault first if your sensitive data goes beyond online checkout:

  • You can stay well under $1,000 a month, and start at $0 on the free tier.
  • You need EU hosting without negotiating an enterprise contract.
  • Cards still reach you by phone, and documents arrive by email.
  • You want to protect customer records, files, or prompts, not only cards.

Basis Theory’s real strength is payments depth. It matters most if you’re a subscription business, marketplace, or fintech where authorization rates and stored-card lifecycle directly move your revenue, and your engineers want to write custom logic against card data.

  • Choose Enigma Vault if: You want PCI scope reduction at a published price, you protect more than cards, or your team has little engineering time.
  • Choose Basis Theory if: Payments are your product and network tokens, routing, card issuing, or agent payments are on your roadmap.

Bottom Line: Basis Theory helps you earn more from each card; Enigma Vault helps you stop holding sensitive data you should not have, for a fraction of the entry price.

Capture, Storage, and Payments: How Enigma Vault and Basis Theory Compare

Card Capture in Web and Mobile Checkout

Basis Theory’s Universal Form Elements are iframe-hosted card fields for web and mobile. They are styled to your brand, keep the card out of your page code, and sit alongside Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Enigma Vault covers checkout with hosted forms and the API. They do the job, but if a pixel-perfect embedded checkout is your top priority, Basis Theory’s Elements are more mature.

Card Capture Over the Phone

Here the comparison flips. Enigma Vault’s Card Vault includes Twilio Pay IVR, so callers enter their card on the phone keypad and agents never hear, type, or record it.

Basis Theory does not package a phone channel. For travel agencies, hotels, clinics, contact centers, and high-value retail, the phone is where card numbers leak most, through call recordings and handwritten notes.

Vault Storage and Token Formats

  • Basis Theory: Stores any payload and lets you shape tokens with expressions, which helps when downstream systems expect a specific format. Access is set per application, and tokens are searchable.
  • Enigma Vault: Issues random tokens with 128-bit entropy, with an option for Luhn-passable tokens if a legacy system checks card formats. CVVs are deleted within 30 minutes.

Every Enigma Vault customer gets its own AES-256 keys, and every request is logged, on every plan including free. If you need custom token formats for complex payment flows, Basis Theory gives you more control.

Customer Records, PII, and PHI

Basis Theory names bank data on all plans and offers PII and PHI compliance options on its Enterprise tier. On the $995 plan, payloads are capped at 1 MB and logs are kept for 24 hours.

Enigma Vault’s Data Vault is built for structured records from the free tier up:

  • AES-256 field encryption for SSNs, passport numbers, and bank details.
  • Exact-match lookup on encrypted values, so you find a record without decrypting it.
  • Batches of up to 5,000 secrets per call.
  • A BAA for healthcare workloads.

If you need to vault personal data without moving to an enterprise contract, Enigma Vault is the simpler path.

Secure File and Document Storage

Basis Theory is designed for small values like cards and bank details, and its entry plan caps payloads at 1 MB. It does not offer a document store.

Enigma Vault’s File Vault stores files up to 5 GB each under envelope encryption, with presigned URLs for controlled upload and download and status updates over WebSocket. ID scans, tax returns, signed contracts, and medical images get the same governance as card data.

Proxy and Outbound Requests

Basis Theory’s Proxy sends and receives sensitive data through any third-party API without it touching your servers, and its Browser Proxy does the same from the client. That flexibility matters if you send card or bank data to many kinds of partners.

Enigma Vault’s gateway proxy is narrower and stricter by default:

  • Your app sends a token, and the proxy checks the destination against a domain allowlist.
  • Scoped OAuth2 credentials limit each client to the vaults and actions it needs.
  • The card number never lands in your logs or monitoring tools.

Reactors and Custom Code on Vaulted Data

Reactors are one of Basis Theory’s most distinctive features. They run your own code against vaulted data inside Basis Theory’s secure environment, so you can transform, validate, or send card data to an unusual partner without it reaching your infrastructure.

They power card issuing, custom partner integrations, and data migrations. Enigma Vault does not run customer code, so if your product depends on that programmability, Basis Theory is the clear choice.

Network Tokens, Account Updater, and PSP Routing

Basis Theory includes a full payment optimization stack:

  • Network tokens and account updater, to keep stored cards working after reissue.
  • 3D Secure and enhanced BIN data.
  • Payment method discovery.
  • Smart and backup routing across payment service providers.

Together they lift authorization rates, which can be worth real money for subscription and marketplace businesses. Enigma Vault charges through the REST gateway you already use (Stripe, Braintree, PayPal, Authorize.net, and others), and switching processors later is a configuration change. It offers none of the optimization tools above; your gateway handles 3DS.

Agentic Payments

Basis Theory offers agentic commerce tools to issue virtual cards and create agentic credentials, and in September 2025 it formed an Agentic Commerce Consortium with partners such as Lithic, Skyfire, and Crossmint. Its $33M Series B in October 2025 was raised partly to build this out.

Enigma Vault approaches AI from the privacy side, not payments, and does not issue payment credentials to agents.

Customer Intake Without Developers

Every Basis Theory workflow is built by your engineers. Enigma Vault’s Customer Vault is a turnkey branded application for staff who do not write code.

  • Collect: No-login intake links for cards, IDs, and documents.
  • Work: A shared inbox with assignment and e-signatures.
  • Protect: Passkeys, enforced MFA, and expiring, password-protected sharing.

PII Protection in LLM Prompts

Basis Theory does not offer prompt protection. Enigma Vault’s NoPII is a reverse proxy that replaces recognized PII with consistent tokens before supported LLM API calls and restores real values in the response, including streamed output.

It supports nine providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, Groq, Together, and Fireworks) and frameworks such as LangChain and Vercel AI SDK. It covers only routes your app sends through it, so test detection on your own data. Starter costs $49 a month for 50M tokens.

Moving From Basis Theory to Enigma Vault

  1. Agree the Card Migration First: Cards move directly between two PCI-compliant providers, and both vendors and your acquirer need to agree the process.
  2. List Your Token Formats: If you shaped tokens with expressions, find every downstream system that depends on that format and choose random or Luhn-passable tokens.
  3. Redesign Reactors and Partner Proxies: Enigma Vault does not run customer code, so each Reactor needs a new home.
  4. Check What Your Gateway Covers: If you rely on network tokens or account updater, confirm your gateway offers them before moving.
  5. Re-Tokenize and Run Both for a Few Weeks: Then cut over.
