What Is Data Tokenization Software for Hotels?

Hotel data tokenization software replaces guest details such as passport numbers, dates of birth, and loyalty numbers with meaningless tokens, so your PMS, CRM, and staff screens hold the token while the real value stays in a protected vault.

Think of a coat check. The guest hands over the coat and gets a numbered ticket; the ticket is useless to anyone who finds it, and only the coat room can match it back (our guide to data tokenization covers the mechanics).

A front desk that stores “tok_8F2K” in place of a passport number works the same way: a stolen PMS export reveals nothing.

How Hotel Data Tokenization Software Handles Registration Rules and Hosting Regions

Registration law turns guest IDs into a retention problem. Germany’s federal services portal cites Article 45 of the Schengen Implementation Convention: foreign guests must complete and sign a registration form in person and show a valid identity document.

The German form records names, date of birth, nationality, address, and passport serial numbers, and must be kept “for one year from the date of arrival,” then destroyed within three months.
