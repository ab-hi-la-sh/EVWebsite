What We Ask Insurance Teams First

We prove one line of business and one packet, never the whole operation. These are the questions we open with:

  • Where do applications arrive today? Email attachments and repeat requests at renewal are the usual start.
  • Who gets the full packet? Often every market, when each carrier needs only part of it.
  • Where does settlement data land? Bank details and tax documents often sit in general claims notes.
  • Does a carrier require its own portal? If so, files leave the vault at that step, and we say so early.
  • Are premiums taken by phone? Twilio Pay capture needs Growth or Business.
  • Any litigation holds? They can limit deletion, so the vault sits beside the claim system of record.

We are also clear on limits: tokenization does not check an application is true, no product makes an agency PCI compliant alone, and NoPII covers only application routes sent through it, not staff pasting claims into a consumer chatbot.
