The alternative is a stitched-together stack. The seams show.

The usual answer to these problems is a stack: a reliability tool, a privacy proxy, a tokenization vendor, an encrypted-storage tool, and a homegrown intake form. Five vendors who have never met, five integrations, five audits, and a gap hiding at every seam.

There is also a quieter mistake: the belief that you can prompt your way to reliable AI or mask your way to private AI. Both treat an infrastructure problem as an application problem.
